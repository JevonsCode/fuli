# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
People managing their local knowledge, collaboration preferences, projects and AI agents.

## Product Purpose
Make durable collaboration context available to agents while keeping ownership, confirmation, scope and history visible to the human.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve existing local-first data, bilingual Chinese/English UI, scoped APIs, capability gates, deep links and audit history. The console includes agents, tasks, knowledge, preferences, employee workbenches, service connections and governance. External employee applications retain their own implementation boundaries.

## Brand Commitments
Retain the FULI name and logo. The approved redesign uses a light, restrained, precise visual language inspired by familiar Apple and Google tools. Prioritize short labels, task clarity, reusable controls, card-based agent browsing and progressive disclosure.

## Product Principles
- Real tasks and pending decisions lead the home page.
- Reduce explanations without hiding essential actions or warnings.
- Display observed states and data; never fabricate activity or progress.
- Reuse one design system across all native surfaces.
- Keep the human authoritative over sharing, permissions and destructive actions.
