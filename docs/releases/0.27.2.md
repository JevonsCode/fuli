# FULI 0.27.2

0.27.2 发在 `latest` 渠道。打开 FULI 先看到的换了：首页是「今天」，带一张复利账单。两台电脑配对之后，相同项目的负责人直接能互相提问，不用再一个个勾。一个本地数据库会自动升级一次（见「数据格式」）。

0.27.2 is on the `latest` channel. What you see first has changed: the home page is Today, with a compounding statement. After two computers pair, the leads of the projects both have can ask each other at once, with nothing to tick. One local store upgrades itself once (see Data format).

## 更新内容 / What's new

首页换成了「今天」：左边一张复利账单告诉你攒下了多少习惯，右边是等你拍板的事和正在干活的助手。两台电脑现在真的能在同一个网里找到对方了（上一版在装了 VPN 的机器上会迷路），配上之后相同的项目自动接通。设置页不再左一块右一块地参差不齐。

The home page is now Today: on the left a compounding statement says how many habits you have saved, on the right what waits for your call and which assistants are working. Two computers on one network now really find each other (the last release got lost on machines with a VPN), and once paired the projects they share connect by themselves. The settings page no longer has ragged columns of unequal height.

## 新功能

- **首页「今天」**：左边固定一张这周的复利账单，右边滚动：等你拍板、今天做了什么、正在干活。没有内容的块不显示。旧的工作台页面去掉了，任务列表还在。
- **复利账单**：你一共攒下多少条习惯和经验，这周有多少次对话带上了它们、被用上多少次、新学到多少。每个数都能点开看它数的是什么、是 FULI 亲眼看到的还是助手自己报告的。量不出来的不写。
- **每日格子图**：账单卡片上点「全部」，看最近 53 周每天的情况，可以切「新学到的 / 确认的 / 带上习惯的对话 / 被用上」，点某一天看那一周的明细。
- **正在干活的客户端标记**：每个任务旁边标出它在哪个客户端里干。记录里有会话、你又开着那个客户端的打开方式时，点一下直接过去。
- **配对即共享相同的项目**：配对确认后，两台电脑上相同的项目（最多 8 个）的负责人直接可以互相提问。确认前会说明这一点；配对后卡片上列出共享了哪些，随时可以取消。组里有第三台电脑、或相同项目超过 8 个时，改成一个按钮一键共享。
- **搜索添加共享项目**：共享列表只显示已共享的和对方也有的项目，其他项目用「添加项目」搜名字加进来。几百个项目的长列表没有了。
- **设置页的布局和宽度由你选**：「布局」可以切「顶部标签」或「左侧分类」；设置 › 系统 › 界面 里的「页面宽度」可以选「舒适」或「铺满」。
- **怎么用**多了两条：把活派给还有额度的其他客户端；客户端额度用完后换一个接着干。
- **让 AI 读复利账单**：新工具 `read_compounding_statement`，返回和页面同一份数字，并说明每一项的来源。

## 改进

- **附近的 FULI 真的找得到了**：发现用的广播以前由系统决定从哪块网卡发，装了 Tailscale、Hyper-V 或 WSL 的电脑会把它发到虚拟网卡上，同一个网里的另一台什么都收不到。现在每块真实网卡各发一份。
- **设置页不再有高度不齐的两栏**：卡片排成一列。
- **界面自己的文字不会被选中**：页面标题、卡片标题、分组小标题、能点的整行，全选时不再高亮；你可能要引用的正文、报错、指纹仍然可选。
- **账单弹框切换上一周 / 这一周不再闪**。
- **会话项目不再出现在共享列表里**；主持配对的电脑不再把自己的指纹显示成「对方指纹」。
- 客户端窗口的名字是「FULI」，不再是「FULI Beta」；设置里说明了「会话打开方式」是什么，「需要你时通知」旁边有 DingDong 的链接。

## 修复

- 「替你做过的改动」这条提醒因为判断条件写错，从来没有出现过。
- 任务的记录里只要有一条是空的，整个任务列表就读不出来。
- 同一个客户端写法不同时（如 `claude-code` 和 `claude_code`），「正在干活」那行出现两个重复的标记。
- 复利账单的数据源全部读不到时，页面显示的是「还没有内容」而不是「读不到」。

## In English

### New

- **Today, the home page.** A fixed card on the left with this week's compounding statement; on the right, scrolling: what waits for your call, what was done today, who is working. A section with nothing to say is not shown. The old workbench page is gone; the task lists remain.
- **The compounding statement.** How many habits and lessons you have saved, how many conversations carried them this week, how often they were used, how many were learned. Every number opens to what it counts and whether FULI saw it or an assistant reported it. What cannot be measured is not written.
- **The statement day by day.** "All" on the statement card shows the last 53 weeks as a calendar, by learned / confirmed / conversations that carried your habits / used; pick a day to see that week's details.
- **Client marks on working rows.** Each task shows which client it runs in; when its records name a session and you turned that client's opener on, one click opens it there.
- **Pairing shares the projects both computers have.** After you confirm a pairing, the leads of the projects both computers have (up to 8) can ask each other. You are told before confirming; afterwards the card lists what was shared and each can be unticked. With a third computer in the group, or more than 8 common projects, one button shares them instead.
- **Add a shared project by search.** The share list shows shared projects and the ones the other computer has too; others are added by name through "Add a project". The list of hundreds of projects is gone.
- **You choose the settings layout and the page width.** "Layout": tabs on top or topics on the left. Settings › System › Interface › "Page width": comfortable or full.
- **How-to** gains two entries: handing work to another client that still has quota, and carrying on in another client when one runs out.
- **An AI can read the statement.** New tool `read_compounding_statement` returns the page's numbers with where each comes from.

### Improved

- **Nearby FULI installs really find each other.** The system used to choose the adapter for discovery; on a computer with Tailscale, Hyper-V or WSL it went out on a virtual adapter and the other computer heard nothing. Each physical adapter now sends its own.
- **No more ragged two-column settings.** Cards sit in one column.
- **The interface's own words are not selectable**: page and card titles, group labels, rows that are links. Text you might quote stays selectable.
- **Switching week in the statement dialog no longer flashes.**
- **Session-only projects are not offered for sharing**; a computer that hosts no longer shows its own fingerprint as the other one's.
- The client window is named "FULI"; Settings says what "Conversation openers" are, and links DingDong next to "Notify when you are needed".

### Fixed

- The "changes made for you" notice never appeared, because it tested the wrong field.
- One empty entry in a task's records made the whole task list unreadable.
- A client written two ways (`claude-code`, `claude_code`) showed two marks on a working row.
- When every source of the statement failed, the page said "nothing yet" instead of "cannot be read".

## 已知限制 / Known limits

- 「配对即共享」需要两台电脑都是 0.27.2。它还没有在真实的两台电脑上走通过，只有自动测试和两轮独立评审。
- 你的电脑是加入方时，「组里还有谁」和「哪些项目相同」是对方电脑说的，本机无法验证。卡片上会写明这一点；不放心可以取消勾选。
- 客户端标记现在是两个字母的缩写，不是各家的图标。任务记录里只有客户端、没有具体会话时，标记不能点。
- 复利账单里「带上习惯的对话」只能往回看大约 180 天；「跨几个客户端」只从这一版开始记。有夏令时的时区，换时那一侧的日子会切偏一小时。
- 首页不再能直接解除任务阻塞，要从任务列表点进去。
- 设置的「左侧分类」布局只在一个标签页、一个窗口尺寸下看过。

- "Pairing shares common projects" needs 0.27.2 on both computers. It has not been run between two real computers yet: automated tests and two independent reviews only.
- When your computer is the one that joined, who else is in the group and which projects match are what the other computer reports; yours cannot check. The card says so, and anything can be unticked.
- Client marks are two-letter abbreviations, not logos. A mark is not a link when the task's records name a client but no session.
- In the statement, "conversations that carried your habits" reaches back about 180 days and "clients" is counted from this release on. In a zone with daylight saving, days on the other side of a clock change are cut an hour off.
- A blocked task can no longer be cleared from the home page; open it from the task list.
- The "topics on the left" settings layout was looked at on one tab at one window size.

## 数据格式 / Data format

没有新增图谱迁移步骤。一个本地数据库在第一次启动时自动升级：召回记录库（1 → 2，多一张表，记录每次任务入口带上了哪些习惯、来自哪个客户端）。升级前会自动备份；升级后退回 0.27.1 需要用那份备份才能打开这个库。局域网圆桌的设置里多了「配对后自动共享」的记录。

No graph migration step is added. One local store upgrades itself at first start: the recall-impressions store (1 → 2, one table recording which habits each task entry carried and from which client). A backup is taken first; going back to 0.27.1 needs that backup to open it. The LAN roundtable settings gain the record of what a pairing shared automatically.

## 安装和升级

需要 Node.js >= 24.12（桌面客户端已内置）。

- **Windows**：在控制台「设置 › 系统 › 版本与更新」点「更新到 v0.27.2」一键更新。也可以下载 `FULI-Setup-0.27.2.exe` 双击（先核对同名 `.sha256`），已装过的会直接更新，数据保留。还可以下载 `fuli-windows-tray-0.27.2.zip`（同样核对 `.sha256`），解压后运行 `Update-Fuli.cmd`。
- **macOS（FULI.app，Apple 芯片）**：在「设置 › 系统 › 版本与更新」点「更新到 v0.27.2」一键更新。第一次安装：下载 `fuli-macos-arm64-0.27.2.dmg`（核对同名 `.sha256`），把 FULI 拖进「应用程序」再打开。
- **npm**（虚拟机、云和服务器）：

```sh
fuli stop
npm install --global fuli-context@latest
fuli setup --yes --connected-only
fuli --version
```

更新后请重启已打开的 Claude Code、Codex 等会话，它们才会用上新版本。

Restart open Claude Code, Codex and other client sessions after updating so they load the new version.
