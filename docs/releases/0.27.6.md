# FULI 0.27.6

0.27.6 发在 `latest` 渠道。一个很小的修复版：放宽「活回你这边做」的每日上限，并把一处误报的「内部错误」改成正常的拒绝。没有数据格式变化。

0.27.6 is on the `latest` channel. A very small fix version: it relaxes the daily limit on work sent to your side and turns one misreported "internal error" into an ordinary refusal. No data format change.

## 更新内容 / What's new

自己的电脑给自己的另一台电脑派活，试了五次、五次都栽在对面的文件夹上，然后 FULI 说：今天的名额用完了，明天请早。这不讲理。现在标成「完全信任」的电脑不再限每天件数，别的电脑也是：不是它的错就不算它的数。

Your own computer sent work to your own other computer, failed five times on a folder over there, and FULI said: that is your five for today, come back tomorrow. That was not fair. A computer you marked fully trusted is no longer held to a daily count, and for any computer, what was not its fault is not counted against it.

## 改进

- **「完全信任」的电脑不限每天件数，也不限每天的唤醒分钟**（原来每天 5 件、60 分钟）。它发来的活也不占别的电脑的名额。只留一条防失控：没处理完的（做完等你看的也算）加上一天内跑失败的，合计最多 10 件；满了就等你看过、或失败的过了一天再继续。
- **不是对方的错就不占名额**：在你这边还没开始执行就失败的活（文件夹不是仓库、Git 步骤失败、没有能用的客户端），不算进对方当天的件数。

## 修复

- 对面的电脑刚启动、还没列出共享的那一小会儿，问它会报「内部错误」。现在如实说「对方现在没有共享这个助手」，并提示过一分钟再试。
- 活跑完之后收尾的 Git 步骤失败时，这次运行的时间没有记下来，也就没算进每天 60 分钟里。现在只要开始跑了就记。

## In English

### Improved

- **A fully trusted computer is held to no daily count and no daily wake minutes** (it was 5 requests and 60 minutes a day). Its requests take nothing from other computers' counts either. One stop stays, against a requester stuck in a loop: ten in all of what is unfinished (results waiting for your look included) and what failed after running in the last day; at ten, the next waits until you have looked or the failures are a day old.
- **What was not the requester's fault is not counted.** Work that failed on your side before it started running (a folder that is no repository, a failed Git step, no client that can run it) is not one of the requester's requests of the day.

### Fixed

- For the moment after the other computer starts and before it lists its shares, asking it answered "internal error". It now says that lead is not shared right now, and to try again in a minute.
- When a Git step failed after the work had run, the run's time was not recorded, so it was left out of the day's 60 wake minutes. It is recorded once a run has started.

## 已知限制 / Known limits

- 两台真电脑之间还没有一件活完整跑完。这一版的两条规则只在自动测试里验过；另外一台 Windows 上走过一次真实更新。macOS 上都还没验。
- 两台电脑都要更新：上限是由收到活的那台电脑执行的。
- 其余限制同 0.27.5。

- No piece of work has run to the end between two real computers yet. This version's two rules were checked by automated tests only; one real update was run on one Windows computer. Nothing was checked on macOS.
- Update both computers: the limit is applied by the computer that receives the work.
- The other limits are those of 0.27.5.
